OpenMix setup

OpenMix is an unofficial, fan-made player for DropMix boards and cards. It ships with none of the game's content: it builds everything from your own copy of the DropMix app. Not affiliated with Hasbro or Harmonix.

1. Get your game files

OpenMix needs the DropMix app (.apk) and its expansion file (.obb) from your phone. OpenMix supports DropMix 1.9.0, the last release.

Android phone with DropMix installed, plugged in by USB with USB debugging on.
